sections:
  - title: "Email is filed to its matter"
    text: "MatterSeal files incoming email to its matter and adds a Gmail label, so Gmail shows the same filing. From the matter you can reply, forward and write new email, and share a draft with a colleague before it goes. When a message comes in that MatterSeal can't place, it goes to a work queue for a person to file."
    image: matter
    alt: "The Mitchell eviction matter page: court caption, stage, items needing attention, email and parties."
  - title: "Court deadlines a person confirms"
    text: "MatterSeal reads court notices in your mail and works out the deadlines that follow from court rules. Each deadline stays marked as proposed until someone at your firm confirms it, and a second person can check it again. If a court deadline isn't acknowledged in time, MatterSeal tells the next person on the firm's list."
    image: filing
    alt: "A court filing matched to its matter, with a proposed response date waiting for a person to confirm it."
  - title: "Citations for a lawyer to check"
    text: "Citations in a filing are found by MatterSeal itself, not by AI. Each one is listed with lookup links for a lawyer to open and mark as checked."
    image: citations
    alt: "A list of citations from a filing, each with links to look it up and a box to tick once a lawyer has opened and read it."
  - title: "AI that waits for your OK"
    text: "MatterSeal can summarize a court filing, answer questions about a matter's documents and draft a reply to a client for a lawyer to edit. Before a document goes to AI, you see what it will cost. The answer points to the passages it relies on. AI never sends email."
    image: answer
    alt: "Ask AI about a notice to vacate, with the passages the answer relies on highlighted in the document."
  - title: "Time, expenses and bills"
    text: "Run a timer or add time on a matter, record expenses, and turn them into a draft bill. A lawyer reads each line as the client will, then approves the bill, sends it as a PDF and records payments."
    image: time
    alt: "The Time page: a paused timer on a matter, and suggested time entries for the day for a lawyer to add, edit or dismiss."
