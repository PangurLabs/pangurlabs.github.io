1. What hosted MatterSeal is

MatterSeal organizes a law practice's email, calendar, documents and court deadlines into matters. In the hosted service:

  • we run the engine for you, in a Google Cloud project we set up for your firm alone, and keep it updated, backed up and watched over;
  • your people use the MatterSeal apps on their computers and phones; and
  • your mail, events and documents stay in your Google account (Gmail or Google Workspace, Calendar and Drive). The engine reads and organizes them through the MatterSeal Google app your people sign in with.

We call all of this the "Service". MatterSeal is a tool for lawyers. It isn't a law firm, it doesn't give legal advice, and using it doesn't create a lawyer-client relationship with us.
