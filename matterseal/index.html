MatterSeal

Matter management for small law firms that run on Google.

MatterSeal is made and hosted by Pangur Labs LLC. It is for solo lawyers and small firms whose email, calendar and documents are in Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive. Gmail and Google Calendar stay exactly as they are, and MatterSeal puts each matter's email, meetings, documents and court deadlines on one page.

A firm signs in with Google in the MatterSeal app for Mac, Windows, iPhone, iPad or Android. Pangur Labs then sets up a Google Cloud project for that firm alone, where the firm's MatterSeal engine and matter records are kept.

What it does

  • Files incoming email to the right matter, with a Gmail label, and lets you reply, forward and send from the matter.
  • Finds court notices in your mail and proposes deadlines from court rules. Each deadline stays proposed until a person confirms it.
  • Shows your Google calendars by matter, and adds the events and deadlines you create or confirm.
  • Keeps each matter's documents in a folder in your Google Drive and previews them in the app.
  • Summarizes court filings and lists their citations for a lawyer to check, and drafts client replies for a lawyer to edit and approve, using the firm's own AI account. AI can be on, ask first, or off for each matter.
  • Runs conflict checks, keeps a Today list and work queue for each person, and backs up every record in the firm's project.

Why MatterSeal asks for access to your Google account

Permission What MatterSeal uses it for
Gmail: read, label and send To show each matter's email, file messages to matters with labels, find court notices and deadlines, and send the replies and new messages a person at your firm writes or approves.
Google Calendar To show each matter's events across your calendars, and add the events and deadlines you create or confirm.
Google Drive, only files MatterSeal creates To keep each matter's folder and documents, and the list of matters.
Your name and email address To sign you in and record who did what in your firm's audit log.

MatterSeal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train generalized AI models. The privacy policy says exactly which services receive which information.
