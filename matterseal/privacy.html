MatterSeal privacy policy (hosted service)

Last updated October 20, 2026 · Draft pending legal review

This is the privacy policy for hosted MatterSeal, and the one the MatterSeal Google app links to. Firms that run MatterSeal in their own Google Cloud project are covered by the self-hosted privacy policy.

Who we are

MatterSeal is made and hosted by Pangur Labs LLC ("Pangur Labs", "we", "us"), a Washington limited liability company. You can reach us about privacy at privacy@pangurlabs.com.

This policy covers hosted MatterSeal, the MatterSeal apps, the MatterSeal Google app you sign in with, and our websites. It's written for the law firms and lawyers who use MatterSeal ("you").

The short version

  • We run MatterSeal for your firm in a Google Cloud project we set up for your firm alone, in the United States. Your mail, events and documents stay in your Google account. The matter records MatterSeal keeps are stored in your firm's project.
  • We process that information only to provide MatterSeal to you, as your service provider, under our data processing agreement.
  • When your firm wants our help, your owner or an admin can give Pangur Labs access for up to 72 hours. It lets us view your engine's logs and health, never your database, documents or mail, and every grant is in your firm's audit log. A person at Pangur Labs opens your firm's information only when your firm asks us to for a specific problem, to stop an active security threat, or when the law requires it, and we tell you when that happens unless the law forbids it.
  • Some information goes to other services when you use the features that need them: part of each new email to our email classifier when mail sorting is on, and the email or filing being worked on to your own AI service on matters where you turn AI on. Each is listed below, with exactly what goes.
  • We never sell your information, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

Your clients' information

The mail, documents and records in MatterSeal often contain your clients' confidential information. You decide what goes into MatterSeal, and you remain responsible to your clients for it under your rules of professional conduct. We treat it as your confidential information, process it only on your behalf, and never contact your clients.

Google data MatterSeal works with

When people at your firm sign in with the MatterSeal Google app, MatterSeal asks Google for these permissions:

Permission What MatterSeal uses it for
Gmail (read, label, send) To show each matter's email, file messages to matters with labels, find court notices and deadlines, and send the replies and new messages a person at your firm approves.
Google Calendar To show each matter's events, add the events and deadlines you create or confirm, and keep them in step with your calendar.
Google Drive (only files MatterSeal creates) To keep each matter's folder and documents, and the list of matters.
Your name and email address To sign you in and record who did what in your firm's audit log.

How it's handled. The sign-in grant that lets the engine reach your Gmail is stored sealed in your firm's project. During setup it waits briefly in our hosting project and is deleted there as soon as your firm's engine has it. Mail, events and documents are read from your Google account when MatterSeal needs them. The engine keeps matter records (which messages, events and documents belong to which matter, deadlines, notes and drafts) and working copies of documents in your firm's project, and backs them up there.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MatterSeal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features you can see and use in MatterSeal.
  • It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.
  • It is transferred to others only as listed under "Services that receive information", as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale of the business with notice to you.
  • No person at Pangur Labs reads it, except when your firm asks us to for a specific support problem, when needed for security (such as investigating abuse), or as required by law.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Services that receive information

Service When What is sent
Google Cloud (our subprocessor) Always Your firm's project runs on it: the engine, matter records, working copies and backups, in Google's us-west1 region (Oregon).
TypeSafe AI (Jev email classifier, our subprocessor) For new incoming mail, only while your firm has mail sorting turned on (the owner or an admin can turn it off in Settings) The sender, subject and the first part of the message body (quoted history and signatures removed, about 1,500 characters), so it can say what kind of email it is. Attachments and court documents are never sent.
Your AI service, on your firm's own key: Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini) or OpenAI Only on matters where AI is turned on, and only one service at a time Only what the feature needs. Filing analysis: the court filing's PDFs and the notice's case details (court, case number, parties, dates). Reply drafts, only after an attorney turns on client replies: the client's email and up to 7 earlier messages in its thread, the matter's name, type, stage, people and upcoming dates, and your firm's style notes and signature. Sorting incoming mail never uses it.
Zoom Only if you connect your own Zoom app The title, time and guests of the meetings you create with a Zoom link.
Court websites When you open a court notice's document link The request to download that document.

Our current subprocessors are on the subprocessor list. We'll tell customers by email at least 30 days before adding one.

About the AI services. Your AI service works under your firm's own account and key, and your agreement with it governs what it does with the data. Under the terms for these paid services, none of the three uses your data to train its models. Each may keep requests for a limited time to watch for abuse. Anthropic deletes API requests within 30 days, but keeps content its safety systems flag for up to 2 years (Anthropic's retention policy). OpenAI says it keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Each can arrange zero data retention with your firm. A key you paste in is stored sealed in your firm's project. If you switch to a different company's service, client reply drafts turn off until an attorney turns them on again, because what clients were told has changed.

Health information. If your firm turns on health information mode in Settings > AI and ethics, no mail goes to the email classifier, and matters you mark as holding health information get no AI unless your firm confirms it has a business associate agreement with its AI service.

Information Pangur Labs uses for its own business

Apart from your firm's information, which we handle only as your service provider, we keep:

  • Your account. The firm's name, the account owner's name and email address, the people invited and when, and the seat count. We use it to set up and run your firm's MatterSeal, bill you and contact your account owner.
  • Usage counts. Seats in use and the number of emails sorted each month, to bill you and apply the sorting limit. They contain no content.
  • Service health. Whether your engine is running, its errors, and how busy it is, so we can keep it working. We don't use these logs to look at what's in your matters.
  • Update checks. The MatterSeal apps check for new versions by downloading a small file from our update storage, which Google Cloud hosts for us. That request carries your IP address and the app version. The apps send no analytics or crash reports.
  • Support you ask for. If you email us, share your screen, send a log or screenshot, or give us Pangur Labs access (up to 72 hours, logs and health only), we see what you share and use it only to help you. We ask you to leave client information out where you can.
  • Email you send us. We keep your message and contact details to reply.
  • Billing. Stripe, our payment processor, collects payment details at checkout and on its billing page, under Stripe's privacy policy. We keep the billing contact, seat count and payment history. We never see full card numbers.
  • Our websites. They use no cookies and no analytics. Our web host sees standard request logs (IP address, page, browser), and fonts loaded from Google Fonts see the same.

Who we share it with

We share information only with our subprocessors and the service providers that help us run the business (Stripe for payments, Fastmail for our email, Google Cloud for hosting), when the law requires it, to protect someone's safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, with notice to you. A buyer must keep our promises in this policy and the data processing agreement.

If anyone asks us for your firm's information under a subpoena or other legal process, we'll send them to you and tell you promptly, unless the law forbids it. If we're compelled to answer, we'll give only what is legally required.

Keeping and deleting data

  • While you subscribe, your firm's information is kept as long as your firm keeps it in MatterSeal. Deleting something in MatterSeal removes it from the engine's records; backups that held it expire on their retention schedule (90 days by default, or the period your firm locked).
  • When you leave, you have 30 days to export everything or move the project into your firm's own Google Cloud organization, after which we remove all our permissions on it. Otherwise we delete the project: we destroy its secrets and backup keys first, confirm in writing, and Google purges it within about 30 days. Locked backups stay until their period ends (90 days by default), unreadable because their keys are gone. Your mail, events and documents stay in your Google account.
  • To end MatterSeal's access to your Google account at any time, remove the MatterSeal app from your Google account permissions.
  • To ask us to delete your firm's information or your own, write to privacy@pangurlabs.com. We'll confirm when it's done.
  • What we keep for our own business (account, billing records, correspondence) we keep while you're a customer and as long afterward as we need for tax, accounting and legal reasons, then delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to privacy@pangurlabs.com. If you're a client or other person whose information a law firm put in MatterSeal, please ask that firm, since it decides how your information is used; we'll pass your request to it. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state's privacy law; we'll honor them. We won't treat you differently for asking.

Security

Each firm has its own Google Cloud project, database, storage and keys. All connections are encrypted with TLS, Google encrypts everything at rest, and keys and sign-in grants are sealed in your firm's project. Staff access works as described above, and everyone at Pangur Labs with access signs in with two-step verification. Our data processing agreement lists our safeguards. If we confirm a security incident affecting your firm's information, we'll tell your account owner within 72 hours.

Children

MatterSeal is a tool for law firms. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For material changes, we'll tell customers by email at least 30 days before they take effect.
