How people agree to texts from a law firm using MatterSeal

Last updated October 30, 2026

A law firm that uses MatterSeal texts only its own clients and others on their legal matters (for example a co-client or a client's family member who is helping with the matter). Nobody is texted until they have agreed in one of the two ways below and the firm has recorded it in MatterSeal. MatterSeal refuses to send a text to anyone without a recorded agreement. Agreeing to texts is never a condition of hiring the firm. Phone numbers are never bought, rented or shared, and texts are never marketing.

1. In writing: engagement letter or intake form

The firm's engagement letter or client intake form (on paper or signed electronically) includes an optional checkbox, separate from the rest of the agreement, with the person's mobile number written next to it:

☐ Yes, [Firm name] may send text messages about my legal matter to my mobile number: ____________. Messages may include appointment and court-date reminders, document requests and replies to my questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop texts at any time, or HELP for help. Agreeing to texts is not a condition of hiring the firm. If the box is left blank, the person is not texted. After the person signs with the box ticked, firm staff open the person's contact in MatterSeal, record their consent to texts, entering how it was given ("Engagement letter, signed 10/30/2026"). MatterSeal stores the date, the method and the staff member who recorded it. The firm keeps the signed document in its matter file.

2. Verbally: in person or on a phone call

When a client or other person on a matter asks to be contacted by text, or staff offer it, staff read this script and wait for a clear yes:

"May [Firm name] text you about your matter at [number]? We'd send things like appointment and court-date reminders, document requests and answers to your questions. How often depends on your matter, and message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or HELP for help. You don't have to agree to work with us."

If the person says yes, staff record it on the contact in MatterSeal the same way (for example "Verbal, phone call 10/30/2026, read texting script"). MatterSeal stores the date, method and staff member. If the person doesn't clearly agree, staff record nothing and the person is not texted.

The first text

The first text the firm sends confirms the agreement and how to stop:

[Firm name]: You're now receiving texts about your legal matter. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.

Stopping texts

Replying STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT) stops all texts from the firm's number at once and is recorded on the contact; MatterSeal won't text that person again unless they reply START. Replying HELP returns: "Texts from your law firm via MatterSeal. For help, call your firm or email support@pangurlabs.com. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg & data rates may apply."

See section 16 of the MatterSeal terms and Text messages in the privacy policy.
