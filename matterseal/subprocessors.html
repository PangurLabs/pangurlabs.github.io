MatterSeal hosted service: subprocessors

Last updated October 20, 2026 · Draft pending legal review

This list is part of our data processing agreement. We'll email each customer's account owner at least 30 days before adding or replacing a subprocessor, and you can object as that agreement explains.

When we host MatterSeal for your firm, we run it in a Google Cloud project we set up for your firm alone. A subprocessor is a company we use that may process your firm's data on our behalf.

Our subprocessors

Company What it does for the hosted service What it receives Where
Google LLC (Google Cloud) Runs your firm's MatterSeal project: the engine (Cloud Run), matter records (Firestore), documents being worked on and backups (Cloud Storage), keys and sign-in grants (Secret Manager, Cloud KMS), background work (Cloud Tasks) and service logs (Cloud Logging) Everything the engine stores or works with for your firm United States (the engine, records and backups are in us-west1, Oregon)
TypeSafe AI (Jev email classifier) Says what kind of email each new message is, only while your firm has mail sorting turned on The sender, subject and the first part of the body (quoted history and signatures removed, about 1,500 characters). No attachments or court documents. United States (being confirmed in our agreement with TypeSafe)
