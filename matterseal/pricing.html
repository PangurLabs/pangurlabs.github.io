Questions

Are there tiers or add-ons?
No. Every user at your firm gets every feature, at the same price.
What happens if we stop paying?
Your records are never deleted or held back for nonpayment. Everyone at the firm can still sign in, read and export everything, and new mail keeps being filed to matters. Changes pause until the subscription is paid.
What if Pangur Labs stops offering MatterSeal?
We'd publish a final license that keeps every self-hosted firm's MatterSeal running for good, with nothing owed. Hosted firms would get at least 90 days' notice where we can, and we'd move each firm's project into its own Google Cloud organization or help it export everything. The terms say this in full.
Can we move from hosted to self-hosted?
Yes. We move your firm's project, with its records and backups, into your firm's own Google Cloud organization, and from then on your firm pays Google directly and Pangur Labs has no access to it. Or you can export everything and we delete the project.
Do we need Google Workspace?
Self-hosted MatterSeal works with Google Workspace or a personal Google account. Hosted MatterSeal starts with Google Workspace firms; personal Gmail accounts follow once Google finishes its review of MatterSeal's access to Gmail.
Which devices does it run on?
Mac today. Versions for iPhone, iPad, Windows and Android are in testing.
