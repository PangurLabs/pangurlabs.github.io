Who we are

MatterSeal is made by Pangur Labs LLC (“Pangur Labs”, “we”, “us”). You can reach us about privacy at privacy@pangurlabs.com.

Pangur Labs provides software you host yourself. Our main product is MatterSeal, email-integrated matter management for law firms. This policy covers the MatterSeal app, the MatterSeal engine software, and this website. It is written for the law firms and lawyers who use MatterSeal (“you”).

The short version

  • MatterSeal runs in a Google Cloud project that you own. Your mail, calendar, documents and matter records are stored in your Google account and your project, not on servers we run.
  • Pangur Labs does not receive, store or read your Gmail, Calendar or Drive data, or the matter records the engine keeps.
  • Some of that data does leave your project. If your firm turns on mail sorting, your engine sends part of each new email to an email classifier (Jev, from typesafe.ai) to sort it. On matters where you turn AI on, it sends the email or filing being worked on to the AI service you choose (Claude, Gemini or OpenAI). Zoom and court websites get a little too, if you use them. These services work under your firm’s own accounts. Each is listed below, with exactly what goes.
  • We never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your clients’ information

The mail, documents and records in MatterSeal often contain your clients’ confidential information. You decide what goes into MatterSeal, and you remain responsible to your clients for it under your rules of professional conduct. Because the engine runs in your own project, Pangur Labs has no technical access to that information. We don’t ask your clients for anything and we don’t contact them.

Google data MatterSeal works with

When you connect your Google account, the MatterSeal engine, running in your own Google Cloud project, asks Google for these permissions:

Permission What MatterSeal uses it for
Gmail (read, label, send) To show each matter’s email, file messages to matters with labels, find court notices and deadlines, and send the replies and new messages a person at your firm approves.
Google Calendar To show each matter’s events, add the events and deadlines you create or confirm, and keep them in step with your calendar.
Google Drive (see and change files, and its activity record) To keep each matter’s folder and documents and the list of matters; to notice documents added, moved or deleted in those folders in Drive; and to find who can open them, so your firm is told when someone outside a matter can reach its documents. MatterSeal uses this permission only for its own folders and what is in them.
Your name and email address To sign you in and record who did what in the audit log.
Google Cloud (during setup, when the setup assistant is released) To create the engine in your own Google Cloud project. The Mac app uses this permission only while you run setup.

This data is processed and stored only inside your Google Cloud project, except for the limited transfers listed under “Services your engine talks to”. It is not sent to Pangur Labs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MatterSeal’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular:

  • Google user data is used only to provide and improve the features you can see and use in MatterSeal.
  • It is not used for advertising, and it is not sold.
  • It is transferred to others only as listed under “Services your engine talks to”, as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or sale of the business with notice to you.
  • No person reads it, except with your permission for a specific support request, when needed for security (such as investigating abuse), or as required by law.
  • It is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Services your engine talks to

Your engine connects to these services using accounts and keys that belong to your firm, kept in your own project. Your agreement with each service governs what it does with the data.

Service When What is sent
Google (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Cloud) Always Everything above. This is where your data lives.
typesafe.ai (Jev email classifier) Only if your firm has mail sorting on (Settings > AI and ethics > Mail sorting), for new incoming mail The sender, subject and the first part of the message body (quoted history and signatures removed, about 1,500 characters), so it can say what kind of email it is. Attachments and court documents are never sent.
Your AI service: the one you pick in Settings > AI and ethics, from Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini) or OpenAI Only on matters where AI is turned on, and only one service at a time Only what the feature needs. Filing analysis: the court filing’s PDFs and the notice’s case details (court, case number, parties, dates). Reply drafts, only after an attorney turns on client replies: the client’s email and up to 7 earlier messages in its thread, the matter’s name, type, stage, people and upcoming dates, and your firm’s style notes and signature. Sorting incoming mail never uses it.
Zoom Only if you connect your own Zoom app The title, time and guests of the meetings you create with a Zoom link.
Court websites When you open a court notice’s document link The request to download that document.

About the AI services. Each runs under your firm’s own account. Claude runs through Anthropic’s API with your key or on Google Vertex AI in your project. If you choose US-only processing in Settings > AI and ethics, Claude requests are processed in the US on either route. Otherwise Anthropic may process them outside the US, though they are stored in the US either way. Gemini runs on Vertex AI in your own Google Cloud project (US multi-region, under Google Cloud’s terms) or through the paid Gemini API with your key. OpenAI runs through its API with your key, and the engine asks OpenAI not to store responses. Under the terms for these paid services, none of the three uses your data to train its models. Each may keep requests for a limited time to watch for abuse. Anthropic deletes API requests within 30 days, but keeps content its safety systems flag for up to 2 years (Anthropic’s retention policy). OpenAI says it keeps abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days. Each can arrange zero data retention with your firm. A key you paste in is stored sealed with your firm’s own encryption key in your project. If you switch to a different company’s service, client reply drafts turn off until an attorney turns them on again, because what clients were told has changed.

Health information. If your firm turns on health information mode in Settings > AI and ethics, no mail goes to the email classifier, and matters you mark as holding health information get no AI unless your firm confirms it has a business associate agreement with its AI service.

Information Pangur Labs does receive

  • License checks. Once a day your engine renews your firm’s license with our license service. It sends only the license ID, the email address of your firm’s account owner, the number of active members, and the app version, plus the IP address any web request carries. It never sends client data, matter names, mail or documents. We use this only to keep your license current and to contact your account owner about the subscription.
  • Update checks. The MatterSeal Mac app checks for new versions by downloading a small file from our update storage, which Google Cloud hosts for us. That request carries your IP address and the app version, as any web request does.
  • Nothing else from the app. Apart from these two checks, neither the app nor the engine sends us usage data, analytics or crash reports.
  • Support you ask for. If you email us, share your screen with us, send us a log or screenshot, or give us access to your Google Cloud project to fix a specific problem, we see what you share and use it only to help you. Access you grant is for that problem only and ends when it’s fixed. We ask you to leave client information out where you can.
  • Email you send us and the pilot. If you write to us or join the pilot, we keep your message and contact details (name, email, firm, practice area) to reply and to run the pilot.
  • Billing. When paid plans open, Stripe, our payment processor, collects payment details at checkout and on its billing page, under Stripe’s privacy policy. We keep the billing contact, seat count and payment history. We never see full card numbers.
  • This website. The site uses no cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without following individual visitors; for each visit Cloudflare receives the page address, the referring site, browser details and IP address. Our web host, GitHub Pages, sees standard request logs (IP address, page, browser).

We use this information only to provide MatterSeal, support you, send service notices, keep our software secure, and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share the limited information we hold only with the service providers that help us run the business (email, hosting of update files and this website, and, later, payments), when the law requires it, to protect someone’s safety or our rights, or as part of a merger or sale of the business, with notice to you.

If anyone asks us for your firm’s data under a subpoena or other legal process, we’ll point them to you, since we don’t hold your matter data, and tell you unless the law forbids it.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Your data in your Google account and your Cloud project is yours to keep or delete. You can remove MatterSeal’s access at any time from your Google account permissions, and delete the engine or the whole Cloud project to remove everything it stored. Its hourly backups live in a storage bucket in your project and are deleted with it.
  • What we hold (correspondence, pilot details, billing records) we keep while you’re a customer and for as long afterward as we need for tax, accounting and legal reasons, then delete it. We delete pilot correspondence when you ask, unless we must keep it by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to privacy@pangurlabs.com. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your state’s privacy law; we’ll honor them. We won’t treat you differently for asking.

Security

All connections between the Mac app, your engine and Google are encrypted with TLS. The Mac app and its updates are signed by Pangur Labs and notarized by Apple. If we learn of a security problem in our software that affects your data, we’ll tell you promptly.

Children

MatterSeal is a tool for law firms. It isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For material changes, we’ll tell customers by email at least 30 days before they take effect.
