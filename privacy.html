Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Pangur Labs LLC (“we,” “us”) makes apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. This policy explains how our apps handle your information. The short version: we don’t collect your personal data.

Data we collect

Our apps do not collect personal data. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your content, and no way to see what you do in the app.

Where your data is stored

Anything our apps store, such as your settings, stays on your device. We cannot access it.

DockStick

DockStick keeps the Dock on the display you choose. It moves the Dock the way you would, by briefly pushing the pointer against the bottom of your display, so it needs Accessibility access, which you approve in System Settings.

  • It uses that access only to move the pointer. It never reads your screen, your keystrokes, or your files.
  • To find out which display the Dock is on, it looks up where the Dock’s window is. It doesn’t read the title or contents of any window.
  • For about a second while it moves the Dock, it ignores your own mouse movement so the gesture isn’t interrupted. That movement isn’t recorded.
  • Its settings, and a small log of when it moved the Dock, are stored only on your Mac.
  • DockStick doesn’t connect to the internet.

Analytics and advertising

Our apps do not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Crash reports and diagnostics

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may share anonymized crash reports and usage statistics with us. We use this only to find and fix problems. You can change this at any time in your device’s privacy settings.

Payments

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details.

Support emails

If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to reply to you. We do not add you to mailing lists or share your message with anyone.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because our apps do not collect personal data, none is collected from users of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the updated version on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email privacy@pangurlabs.com.
