Frequently asked questions

How do I download an app again, for example on a new Mac?

Apps you’ve bought belong to your Apple Account, so you can download them again at no charge. Open the App Store, sign in with the Apple Account you bought the app with, and find it in your purchases.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

DockStick

Where do I give DockStick Accessibility access?

In System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, open Accessibility (macOS 13 to 26) or Device Control and Data Access (macOS 27), and switch on DockStick. DockStick’s welcome window and settings have a button that starts this for you.

DockStick is switched on there, but still says it needs access.

Switch DockStick off and on again in the same list. macOS sometimes needs this after an app is updated.

Which display does DockStick keep the Dock on?

Your largest external display, unless you choose another one under Target display in DockStick’s settings.

The Dock didn’t move back.

Check that “Keep the Dock on the target display” is on in DockStick’s settings, then choose Move Dock Now from the pushpin icon in the menu bar. DockStick also checks automatically after your Mac wakes or you unlock it. If the Dock still won’t move, email us and include how your displays are arranged (System Settings > Displays > Arrange).

Why did my pointer move by itself for a moment?

That’s DockStick moving the Dock. It briefly pushes the pointer against the bottom of your display, the same gesture you would use, then puts the pointer back where it was. It takes about a second.

How do I start DockStick when I log in?

Turn on Start at login in DockStick’s settings.
