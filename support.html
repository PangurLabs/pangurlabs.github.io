Frequently asked questions

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app and go to Settings > Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to make the original purchase.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. You can request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
